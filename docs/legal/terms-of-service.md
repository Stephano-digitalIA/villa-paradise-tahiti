### 2.3 Seasonal rates and minimum stay

Nightly rates apply to the entire villa (sleeps up to 8) and vary by season.
The rate shown and itemized at checkout is always final:

- **Low season** (May to June and October to November): USD 590 per night;
- **High season** (July to September and December through early January): USD

- **Peak holidays** (Christmas week, New Year, and Easter): USD 1,290 per
  night.

A minimum stay of five (5) nights applies in low and high season, and seven (7)
nights during the peak holiday period. A one-time cleaning fee is added to

### 2.4 Long-stay discount

Reservations of fourteen (14) nights or more automatically receive a ten
percent (10%) discount on the accommodation portion of the booking, that is,
the villa nightly subtotal together with the cleaning fee. The discount is
calculated and shown at checkout before the deposit is determined, so the
thirty percent (30%) deposit and the remaining balance are both computed on the
already-discounted total. The long-stay discount does not apply to optional
experiences or third-party excursions and may not be combined with any other
